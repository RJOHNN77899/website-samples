Good to know

Questions we get a lot

Do I need to book?

No. Walk-ins are welcome whenever a chair is free. Booking just means you won't wait, which helps most on Saturdays.

How do I pay?

Cash or any major card, including tap to pay. Tips can go on the card.

Do you cut kids' hair?

Yes, any day we're open. Dee is especially good with first haircuts.

What if I'm running late?

Your chair is held for 10 minutes. Running later than that? Call and we'll fit you in if we can.
