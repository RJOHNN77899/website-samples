FAQ

Questions homeowners ask us

Is the estimate really free?

Yes. The site visit and the written estimate cost nothing, and there's no pressure to sign.

Do you pull permits?

Yes. When a job needs a permit, we apply for it and book the inspections, and the cost is shown in your estimate.

How does payment work?

Small jobs are paid on completion. Larger projects have a deposit and payments tied to finished stages, all written into the estimate before you sign.

Can you work with my insurance after a storm?

We document the damage with photos and measurements and can meet your adjuster on site. Your claim stays between you and your insurer.

What does the warranty cover?

Our workmanship, for two years from the walkthrough. Materials carry their makers' warranties, which we register for you.
