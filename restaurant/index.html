Our kitchen

Recipes worth the wait

Kettle & Cane started with a family cookbook and a cast-iron kettle that's older than the building. We cook the way those recipes ask: pork in the smoker overnight, greens on the stove all morning, biscuits rolled by hand.

We buy from farms within a couple of hours of Tuscaloosa whenever we can, and the menu shifts a little with the seasons.
